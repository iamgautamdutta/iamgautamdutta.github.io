RentndBuy

A trust-focused rental and sale platform for Palam, Delhi, connecting property owners and tenants directly with no broker commission. Owners can list rental or sale properties with rent, deposit, size, furnishing and location details. Tenants can browse for free or post an “I’m Looking For” requirement.

Phone numbers stay hidden until sign-in, and every contact unlock is logged to deter scraping and broker abuse. Browsing is free; posting costs ₹49/month for owners and ₹29/month for tenants.
